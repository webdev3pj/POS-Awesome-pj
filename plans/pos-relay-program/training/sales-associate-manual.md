# Sales Associate Manual (SA)

## Your Mission
Create the customer order token quickly and accurately, then hand off to cashier.

## Before You Start
1. Log in with your SA user.
2. Confirm top status chips:
   - `Relay Online (LAN)` should be green.
   - `Cloud Online` may be green or not; relay is the main local continuity path.
3. Confirm you are in SA role (not cashier/picker/dispatch).

## Main Workflow
1. Select customer.
2. Add items and quantities.
3. Confirm totals with customer.
4. Click `Save/New` to create the Sales Order token.
5. In token dialog, click `Print Token Slip`.
6. Give printed token to customer and tell them to present it at cashier.

## Buttons You Use Most
- `Save/New`: create the token/order.
- `Print Token Slip`: print customer token.
- `Select S.O`: normally not used by SA.
- `PAY`: SA does not collect payment.

## Simplified SA Mode (If Enabled by Manager)
If profile toggle `Simplified SA and Cashier UI` is ON:
- SA will not see `Held`, `Return`, and `PAY`.
- This is expected and helps prevent wrong actions.

## What You Must Not Do
1. Do not collect payment.
2. Do not submit invoice.
3. Do not release goods.

## If Something Goes Wrong
1. Token not printing:
   - Try `Print Token Slip` again.
   - If still failing, capture order number and inform cashier manually.
2. Relay chip not green:
   - Inform supervisor/IT immediately.
3. Cannot save token:
   - Verify customer and at least one item are present.
   - Retry once, then escalate.

## End of Shift
1. Ensure all created tokens are visible in order monitor.
2. Handover any special customer notes to cashier/supervisor.
